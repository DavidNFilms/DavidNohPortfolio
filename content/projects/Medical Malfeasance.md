A group documentary made for National History Day in 2025 that took **1st place
at the Long Island regional** and **1st place in New York State** before
finishing in the **national top 25%**. *Medical Malfeasance* follows the
thalidomide disaster — a drug sold as a safe sedative that left thousands of
children with severe birth defects — and the regulatory reckoning it forced on
the pharmaceutical industry. Built as a two-person edit, it leans on archival
footage, period print, and expert interviews to hold a heavy story steady.
