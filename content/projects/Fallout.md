A documentary short made for National History Day in 2024 that took **1st place
at the Long Island regional** and **2nd place in New York State** before
finishing in the **national top 20%**. *Fallout* traces what happened to the Soviet Union's
nuclear technology once the state that built it dissolved — the warheads,
reactors, and scientists left behind, and the scramble to account for them. The
cut leans on archival footage and a steady narration track to keep a dense,
document-heavy history moving.
