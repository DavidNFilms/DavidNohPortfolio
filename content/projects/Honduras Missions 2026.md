Returning to Honduras for a second year with Promise Church, this film
picks up where last year's story left off. Where the 2025 trip was about
breathing life back into an abandoned school building, this one follows
us bringing awe and wonder once again to this place.

It's the latest chapter in an ongoing effort to document these trips not
just as a record of the work done, but as a story that keeps unfolding
year over year.
