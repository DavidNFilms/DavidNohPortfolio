Who I am

Hi! I'm David, a multidisciplinary creator blending design, technology, and storytelling to build immersive experiences. With over 8 years of experience in video editing and videography and 9 years in photography, I've honed my ability to capture emotion, craft narratives, and connect audiences across platforms.

I'm currently studying Integrated Design & Media at NYU Tandon with a minor in Business of Media, Entertainment, and Technology. My passion lies in the intersection of augmented reality, immersive media, and creative expression. I believe in using emerging tech to shape how we experience the world.

From launching social media campaigns and coaching youth sports, to winning awards in visual arts and collaborating on XR experiences, I'm always seeking new ways to push boundaries and tell meaningful stories.

Let's connect! Whether you're in tech, media, education, or the creative world, I'm always excited to meet people who are building the future.
